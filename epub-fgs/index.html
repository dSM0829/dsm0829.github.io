EPUB Reader: Open & Read Books. Background read aloud

The user opens a book and taps Read aloud. The app reads the book with text to speech and highlights the sentence being read. When the user leaves the app or turns the screen off, reading continues through a media playback foreground service. A media notification titled "Reading a book" shows previous sentence, pause and next sentence controls. Reading stops when the user taps Pause or the close button. The video was recorded on a Galaxy Tab without sound capture.
